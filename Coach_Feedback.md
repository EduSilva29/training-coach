### 2. Tactical Focus for Next Session: Alactic Neuromuscular Priming

You need high-velocity stimulus without metabolic exhaustion. Do not run sustained hard intervals yet; re-engage your fast-twitch fibers via pure speed and low lactate accumulation.

#### **Workout: Aerobic Run + Pure Speed Hill Sprints**
* **Warm-up / Base:** 6–8 km easy at 5:15–5:30/km (HR strictly under 140 bpm).
* **Neuromuscular Block:** 
  * **6 to 8 × 15 seconds steep uphill sprints (6–8% grade) at maximum velocity.**
  * **Recovery:** 90 seconds to 2 minutes of **walking/complete rest** between reps (HR must drop below 120 bpm before the next rep).
* **Cool-down:** 1–2 km jog.

#### **Key Execution Metrics:**
1. **Cadence Target:** Drive cadence above **190–195 spm** on the hill reps. Focus on rapid knee-drive and forefoot strike.
2. **Ground Contact Time:** Minimize contact time—treat the ground like hot coals. 
3. **Metabolic Control:** Keep reps strictly at $\le 15$ seconds. Any longer introduces excessive acidosis, shifting the session from neuromuscular activation into glycolytic fatigue, which blunts recovery ahead of your December 13 race.
